Batch 100 / complete worked tutorial / synthetic data

Import. Inspect. Explain. Export.

One small dataset, one reproducible question: how does revenue vary by record, and what must we check before sharing the pattern?

1 / Start with a transparent assessment

Open the 49-point exploration example. Its weakest readiness area is data quality. Check missing values and types instead of claiming a business outcome. Synthetic CSV, a personal audience, and today produce a 15-minute learning plan—not a production rollout.

2 / Import a deliberately imperfect sample

Download messy CSV or sparse JSON. Open ASF Studio in this tab and import it. Expect 6 rows, 5 fields, and 1 missing cell. The JSON omits one revenue key; normalization makes it null.

The import lab offers a no-download trial and explicit workspace handoff. Limits: 2 MiB, 10,000 rows, 50 columns; no nested JSON.

3 / Separate completeness from numeric coverage

Open Analytics in the same tab. There are 30 cells, with 29 nonmissing: 29 ÷ 30 × 100 = 96.67% completeness. “pending” in orders is present text, excluded from numeric statistics.

  • Revenue: 5 numeric values, 1 missing; mean 138, median 150, minimum −30, maximum 240.
  • Orders: 5 numeric values, 1 text value, no missing; numeric mean 13.8. This is not a mean of all six orders.
  • Zero orders is numeric and present. Negative revenue is an adjustment, not a missing value.

4 / Make the filter scope explicit

Search North: 3 rows, 100% completeness, revenue mean 80. Search South: 3 rows, 93.33% completeness, revenue mean 225 over only 2 numeric revenues. Reset before comparing with the full dataset. Search is a substring across every cell, not SQL.

Click Visualize filtered data for a North-only chart. That explicitly replaces the shared workspace with three rows; ordinary navigation does not apply the filter. No matches must leave the workspace unchanged.

5 / Choose a chart and disclose exclusions

In Visualizer, use revenue by month as a bar chart. May’s negative bar must extend below zero. With all six rows, five revenue values produce marks; missing March is excluded. Bars are individual records, not grouped totals.

Lines follow file order, not automatic date sorting. Missing-record gaps break the line. Revenue-versus-orders scatter has four valid numeric pairs: missing March revenue and text April orders exclude two records. The plotted-data table is a nonvisual alternative.

Caption example: “Synthetic monthly revenue records; five of six values plotted, one missing revenue excluded. May includes a −30 adjustment. File order is preserved; no regional aggregation or causal claim.” Add actual units when using approved data.

6 / Export and inspect locally

Export filtered CSV and summary JSON from Analytics, and SVG from Visualizer. Check matched rows, units, exclusions, and caption before sharing. Formula-like CSV text is neutralized on export; original files are never edited.

7 / Finish deliberately

Clear the saved ASF workspace. Retain only approved local files and record one observation plus one limitation. Discovery email controls prepare drafts only; you must review and click Send. This tutorial never contacts an email provider.
