A repeatable, local-first workflow

  1. Choose one question. Use Discovery to identify a goal and the weakest readiness area. Its score is guidance, not a guarantee.
  2. Start with a synthetic or permitted export. Follow the ASF quickstart. Import at most 2 MiB, 10,000 rows, and 50 columns.
  3. Check before interpreting. Read the completeness and statistics guide. Missing values, mixed types, and filter choices can change a conclusion.
  4. Choose a chart that fits. Follow the bar, line, and scatter guide. Bars are individual records, lines follow input order, and scatter needs numeric pairs.
  5. Review and share deliberately. Export locally. Inspect your output before copying it into an email or another document.

Privacy & limitations

All four tools process their inputs in the browser. There are no runtime API calls, third-party fonts, libraries, analytics, or upload endpoints. Hosting still requires a normal page request to GitHub Pages; this is not an offline-availability guarantee.

ASF uses same-tab sessionStorage to hand datasets between its apps. Every project on this Pages origin can technically access the same origin's storage. Do not use confidential or sensitive information; clear the ASF workspace when finished. Browser session restoration is browser-dependent.

Discovery keeps answers only in page memory. Copy/download saves results where you choose. Clicking a Gmail or mail-app draft button passes the report to that application/provider. You must review the draft and click Send; delivery is not guaranteed or confirmed by this site.

These tools are exploratory aids, not professional advice, a secure data vault, or proof of business readiness. Source-only projects may have their own dependencies and setup requirements; they are outside the dependency-free browser runtime.
